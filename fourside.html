Fourside

The place everyone ends up.

Welcome to Eagleland's biggest city. Catch a show at the Topolla Theater, browse the department store, or watch the skyline light up from a café window. Plan for at least a full day. There's plenty to see, and it's easy to lose track of time.

Fourside is also home to the Monotoli Building, the tallest tower in the city. A few years ago, someone borrowed the helicopter from its roof and never brought it back.

Something is always happening here.

Fourside has a population of 313,003 people, average rainfall of 32 inches and 10 parks.

Travel advisory: Moonside

Some visitors report a strange, neon side of the city that isn't on any map. Directions there don't make sense, and the locals don't give straight answers, even to simple questions.

The Tourism Board does not recommend it.

Things to see

  • Monotoli Building

    Fourside's tallest tower, 48 floors high, with a private rooftop helipad (currently empty).

  • Topolla Theater

    Live music and shows most nights. The Runaway Five are a local favorite, so book ahead.

  • Grand Department Store

    Floor after floor under one roof. Pick a meeting spot in case your group splits up.

  • The Museum

    Dinosaur skeletons and a gift shop. A reliable rainy-day option.

Eat and stay

  • Jackie's Café

    The best coffee this side of the desert. Please don't ask about the back room.

  • Fourside Bakery

    Fresh croissants every morning.

  • Enrich Grand Hotel

    Double burgers available in the lobby for late arrivals.

  • Mach Pizza

    Delivery only. Phone in your order from almost anywhere and they'll come find you.

Know before you go

  • Expect delays on the desert highway from Threed. Unauthorized tunneling has been reported.
  • The department store is fully back on the power grid. Pick a meeting spot anyway.
  • The city sewers are closed to the public. There's nothing down there worth seeing.
  • The mayor's office has been much friendlier lately. Visitors are welcome to say hello.
