Onett

Every great trip needs a beginning.

Onett is a quiet hilltop town, and the hometown of a boy named Ness. One night, a meteorite crashed on the hill above his house. He went up to take a look, and didn't come home for quite a while.

Most days, nothing much happens here.

At a glance

Population:
3,500 (+2 dogs)
Temperature:
72°F average
Rainfall:
10 inches a year

Listen to Onett

Press play to hear the official music of Onett.

The Tourism Board describes it as "the feeling of riding your bike home before dinner."

Things to see

The Hilltop

Site of the famous 199X meteorite landing, now Onett's most popular viewpoint.

Public Library

Quiet, friendly, and free. Ask at the front desk for a town map. You'll be glad you did.

The Arcade

A lively spot for an evening out. Popular with local teens.

Giant Step

A cave in the northern hills, for experienced hikers only.

Eat and stay

The Burger Shop

Burgers, fries, and a booth by the window.

Mach Pizza

Delivery available to almost anywhere.

Onett Bakery

Fresh bread every morning.

The Hotel

Clean rooms and a good night's sleep.

Know before you go

  • The road south to Twoson is sometimes closed by police roadblocks. Check with the Tourism Board before you travel.
  • Local wildlife has calmed down considerably. Dogs, crows, and snakes are back to minding their own business.
  • The local drugstore has snacks, travel supplies, an ATM, and a phone. Stock up before you head out of town.
  • The family living in the house beside the Hilltop has had a busy few years. Please respect their privacy.
